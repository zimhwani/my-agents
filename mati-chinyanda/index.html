About me

Hi, I'm Mati.

I'm a speaker and MC based in Melbourne. I was born in Zimbabwe, and I've spent the last ten years in rooms full of people, usually with a microphone in my hand.

I co-founded a podcast because the conversations in my group chat were too good to keep private, and hosting grew out of the same instinct. I like a room with energy in it, and I'm happy to be the person who brings it. Give me a stage, a run sheet and an audience, and I'll make sure everyone leaves feeling like they were part of something.

Three women. One group chat. No filter.

Meet Mati

Warm on the mic. Sharp on the run-sheet.

Mati Chinyanda is a Melbourne-based speaker, MC and event producer with Zimbabwean roots and a fashion founder's instinct for a room.

She built her own stages before she was invited onto anyone else's: a multicultural runway platform, a fashion label and blog, and a podcast that turned the group chat into a public conversation. On stage that shows up as energy you can feel from the back row, timing that keeps a program honest, and the production sense to know when a moment needs holding.

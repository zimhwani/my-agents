The podcast

What Left The Group Chat

Three women. One group chat. No filter. I co-host this one with Tahj and Jill. One of us is Zimbabwean, one is African American, one is Zambian, and all of us are figuring out life in Australia: work, love, money, beauty, motherhood and everything in between.

About the show

The conversations we used to keep to ourselves.

Every woman I know has a group chat where the real talk happens. The job that wants you to shrink. The beauty standards you inherited. Whether to stay in Australia or go home. Who's carrying the mental load. That's the show.

We launched in October 2025, we record in Melbourne, and we're now into season two. It's frank, it's funny, and it's rooted in what it's like to be an African woman building a life here. If you want to know how I hold a room, this is the easiest place to find out.

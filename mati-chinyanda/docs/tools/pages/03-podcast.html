About the show

Where laughter, vulnerability and real conversations collide.

The group chat is where women say the real thing. What Left The Group Chat puts those conversations on the record: the job that asks you to shrink, the beauty standards you inherit, the migrant maths of staying or going, the mental load, and the friendships that keep you upright.

Launched in October 2025, the show is co-founded and co-hosted by Mati with Tahj and Jill. It's frank, funny and rooted in the African diaspora experience in Australia, and it has become the clearest window into how Mati holds a room: curious, quick and generous.

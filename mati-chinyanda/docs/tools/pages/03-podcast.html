What Left The Group Chat

About the show

The conversations we used to keep to ourselves.

Every woman I know has a group chat where the real talk happens. The job that wants you to shrink. The beauty standards you inherited. Whether to stay in Australia or go home. Who's carrying the mental load. That's the show.

We launched in October 2025 and we record in Melbourne. It's frank, it's funny, and it's rooted in what it's like to be an African woman building a life here. If you want to know how I hold a room, this is the easiest place to find out.

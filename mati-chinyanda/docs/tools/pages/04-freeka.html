Why I started it

The talent was here. The stage wasn't.

Back in 2015 I knew a lot of designers, dancers, artists and musicians in Melbourne who had the work but no runway to show it on. So I built one.

The first show went far better than we expected. The second one was staged for Melbourne Spring Fashion Week in 2016, with a cast drawn from every corner of the city's multicultural creative scene, and The Age came along to cover it. FreekÀ has always been a platform first and a show second. My job is to build the room and then hand the spotlight to the people in it.

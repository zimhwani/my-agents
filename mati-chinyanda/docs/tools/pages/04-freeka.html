Express : Your : Self

FreekÀ Runway is a multi-culturally inspired runway show celebrating how far ethnic communities have come within Melbourne's creative space, and a platform for emerging creatives to express themselves through fashion, dance, art and music. Founded by Mati Chinyanda.

FreekÀ

Runway · Melbourne · est. 2015

The idea

Build the stage, then give it away.

Mati started FreekÀ because the creatives she knew in Melbourne, designers, dancers, artists, musicians from every community, had the work but not the runway.

The first show in 2015 was, in the platform's own words, an unexpected success. The second was staged for Melbourne Spring Fashion Week in 2016, with a cast of emerging designers, performers and models drawn from the city's multicultural creative scene. FreekÀ is a platform first and a show second: the founder's job is to build the room, then hand the spotlight to the people in it.

Value 01

Platform emerging creatives

First shows, first collections, first stages. FreekÀ exists to give people a professional runway before the industry notices them.

Value 02

Fashion, dance, art & music

A runway that moves like a show. Looks walk to live performance; art and music share the bill with fashion.

Value 03

Community first

Multicultural by design, with a crowd that looks like Melbourne. Every credit is shared, every collaborator named.

Event production

Shows and events, produced properly.

Mati combines a founder's creative vision with the discipline of a career in program management. Bring her in for the whole show or one part of it.

  1. 01

    Creative direction

    Concept, narrative and look of the show or event, from mood boards to the final walk.

  2. 02

    Runway & show production

    Venue, staging, lighting and sound, rehearsal schedules, backstage flow and the run-sheet that holds it all together.

  3. 03

    Casting & talent

    Designers, models, dancers, musicians and hosts sourced from Melbourne's multicultural creative community.

  4. 04

    Show calling & stage management

    Calm on comms, precise on cues. Mati can call the show, host it, or both.

  5. 05

    Brand activations & cultural events

    Launches, community celebrations, cultural programs and brand moments that need a real audience and a real point of view.

  6. 06

    Program management

    Budgets, stakeholders, timelines and reporting, from years running programs inside a major university.

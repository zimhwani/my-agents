About

Mati.

Speaker, MC, podcaster and founder. Zimbabwean by heritage, Melbourne by home, and at her best with a mic in one hand and a run-sheet in the other.

The story

She built the rooms first.

Mati Chinyanda's career has one through-line: making space for people to be seen, then holding that space with warmth and precision.

It started in fashion. Under the name L'entendre ("Raw. Style. Truth. Love."), she wrote about style and identity from the African diaspora in Australia, ran a small label, and was nominated for Fashion Blogger of the Year at the Zimbabwe International Women's Awards in 2016. She also led FashionOne, and wrote for Salt Magazine, the magazine of African-Australian life.

In 2015 she founded FreekÀ Runway, a multicultural runway show and platform for emerging creatives in Melbourne. The debut was an unexpected success; the second edition was staged for Melbourne Spring Fashion Week in 2016 with designers, dancers, artists and musicians from across the city's communities. Its motto, Express : Your : Self, is still the best description of what she wants an audience to feel.

Alongside the creative work, Mati spent years in program management at Monash University, running education programs and the budgets, stakeholders and timelines that come with them. It's why event organisers get a host who is as calm about the run-sheet as she is electric on stage.

In October 2025 she co-founded What Left The Group Chat, a podcast with Tahj and Jill: three bold Black women on work, beauty, migration, the mental load and friendship, on the record. In 2026 she takes the stage as MC of the Women of Colour Melbourne Annual Gathering.

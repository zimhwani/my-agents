About

Hi, I'm Mati.

Speaker, MC and podcaster. Born in Zimbabwe, at home in Melbourne, and happiest with a mic in one hand and a run sheet in the other.

My story

I've always ended up holding the room.

Everything I've done comes back to one thing: making space for people to be seen, and then making sure the day runs well once they're in it.

I started out writing. I wrote about style and identity as an African woman in Australia, contributed to Salt Magazine, and learned early that the best thing you can do for an audience is tell them the truth with a bit of warmth. That instinct is still the whole job.

Alongside all of that I spent years in program management at Monash University, looking after education programs and the budgets, stakeholders and timelines that come with them. That's why event organisers get someone who's as calm about the run sheet as she is lively on stage.

In October 2025 I co-founded What Left The Group Chat with Tahj and Jill. It's a podcast about work, beauty, migration, motherhood, the mental load and friendship, told by three Black women living in Australia, and it's now in its second season. And in October 2026 I'll be hosting the Women of Colour Melbourne Annual Gathering as MC.

About me

Hi, I'm Mati.

I'm a speaker, MC and event producer based in Melbourne. I was born in Zimbabwe, I came up through fashion, and I've spent the last ten years putting other people on stage.

I started FreekÀ Runway because Melbourne had a lot of talented designers, dancers and artists with nowhere to show their work. Then I co-founded a podcast because the conversations in my group chat were too good to keep private. Hosting grew out of both. I like a room with energy in it, and I'm happy to be the person who brings it.

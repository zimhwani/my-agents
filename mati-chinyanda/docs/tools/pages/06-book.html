FAQ

Good questions.

How are fees set?

By format, duration, preparation and audience. Community and not-for-profit rates are available. A budget range on the form gets you a faster, better-fitted proposal.

Does Mati travel?

Yes: Australia-wide and internationally from Melbourne. Travel and accommodation are itemised in the proposal.

Can she host and produce the same event?

Often the best outcome. Mati can creative-direct and produce a show or event, then host it, so the vision survives contact with the run-sheet.

What lead time do you need?

Four to six weeks for a keynote; MC bookings can often be confirmed faster. Ask about short notice.

What about AV and tech?

Lapel or handheld mic, a confidence monitor for long programs, and a briefing call the week before. Full rider supplied on booking.

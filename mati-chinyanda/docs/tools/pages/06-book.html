Things people usually ask.

What do you charge?

It depends on the format, how long I'm on stage, the prep involved and the size of the audience. I have community and not-for-profit rates. A budget range in the form gets you a faster, better-fitted proposal.

Do you travel?

Yes, anywhere in Australia and overseas, from Melbourne. Travel and accommodation are itemised in the proposal.

Can you host and produce the same event?

Yes, and it's often the best way to do it. I can creative-direct and produce a show or event and then host it, so the idea survives contact with the run sheet.

How much notice do you need?

Four to six weeks for a keynote. MC bookings can usually be confirmed faster. If it's short notice, ask anyway.

What about AV and tech?

A lapel or handheld mic, a confidence monitor for long programs, and a briefing call the week before. I'll send a full rider when we confirm.

Signature talks

Six conversations worth booking.

Each talk is tailored to your audience and can run as a keynote, a fireside or a facilitated session. Open one to see the takeaways.

Your authentic self at the J.O.B.

Code-switching, belonging and the quiet cost of shrinking at work. What it actually takes for people of colour, migrants and women to show up whole, and what leaders can change tomorrow.

Best for: corporate conferences, leadership offsites, inclusion programs.

Becoming our own standard of beauty

From a fashion blog and label to founding a multicultural runway: representation, self-image and fashion as self-expression, with the receipts.

Best for: fashion and beauty industry events, women's networks, creative festivals.

Should we stay or go? Migrant stories in Australia

Home, ambition and the in-between. A frank, funny look at building a life between two cultures and the decisions no one warns you about.

Best for: community gatherings, multicultural organisations, universities.

The mental load

Womanhood, ambition and the invisible work of carrying it all. Honest tools for the women holding the calendar, and for the partners and teams around them.

Best for: women's summits, wellbeing programs, International Women's Day.

Build the room you couldn't find

The FreekÀ Runway founder story: what it takes to turn an idea into a stage, put emerging creatives on it, and keep the platform bigger than the founder.

Best for: entrepreneurship and creative-industries events, founder programs.

The friendship hustle at our big age

Community, connection and keeping people close when life gets busy. Warm, practical and very quotable.

Best for: community events, culture-led brands, podcasts and live shows.

For planners

The practical bits.

Where is Mati based, and does she travel?

Melbourne / Naarm. She travels Australia-wide and internationally; travel and accommodation are quoted with the proposal.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on format, duration, preparation and audience. Share a budget range on the enquiry form and Mati will come back with a clear proposal, including community and not-for-profit rates.

How much lead time do you need?

Four to six weeks is ideal for a keynote; MC bookings can often be confirmed faster. Short-notice requests are welcome, just ask.

What does Mati need on the day?

A run-sheet, a briefing call the week before, a lapel or handheld mic and a confidence monitor for longer programs. Headshots, bios and an intro script are supplied ahead of time.

Does she do virtual events?

Yes. Mati delivers virtual and hybrid sessions from a studio setup with professional audio.

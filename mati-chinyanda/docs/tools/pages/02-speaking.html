Your authentic self at the J.O.B.

Code-switching, fitting in and what it costs to make yourself smaller at work. What it takes for people of colour, migrants and women to turn up as themselves, and what leaders can change tomorrow.

Good for: corporate conferences, leadership offsites, inclusion programs.

Becoming our own standard of beauty

I went from a fashion blog to a label to founding a runway show. This one is about representation, self-image and dressing for yourself, with the receipts.

Good for: fashion and beauty events, women's networks, creative festivals.

Should we stay or go? Migrant stories in Australia

Home, ambition and the in-between. A frank and funny look at building a life between two cultures, and the decisions nobody warns you about.

Good for: community gatherings, multicultural organisations, universities.

The mental load

The invisible work of running a life, a family and a career at the same time. Practical, honest and useful for the women carrying it and the partners and teams around them.

Good for: women's summits, wellbeing programs, International Women's Day.

For planners

The practical stuff.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Melbourne. I travel anywhere in Australia and overseas. Travel and accommodation are itemised in the proposal so there are no surprises.

What do you charge?

It depends on the format, how long I'm on stage, the prep involved and the size of the audience. Put a budget range in the enquiry form and I'll come back with a clear proposal. I have community and not-for-profit rates.

How much notice do you need?

Four to six weeks is ideal for a keynote. MC bookings can usually be confirmed faster. If it's short notice, ask anyway.

What do you need on the day?

A run sheet, a briefing call the week before, a lapel or handheld mic, and a confidence monitor for longer programs. I'll send headshots, a bio and an intro script well ahead of time.

Do you do online events?

Yes. I run online and hybrid sessions from a studio set-up with proper audio.

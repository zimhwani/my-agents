## 0. Read this first: method, limits and confidence

**Method.** Public web search (Google-index snippets via WebSearch) across ~45 queries covering her name, the podcast, FreekÀ Runway, L'entendre, FashionOne, Monash, ZIWA, and social platforms.

**Hard limitation.** The research environment's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches to *every* primary source (Salt Magazine, freekarunway.com, Apple/Spotify/Amazon/Audible/Castro, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn/ZoomInfo, WordPress, Monash, Getty, Wayback Machine). Every fact below therefore comes from search-engine snippets of those pages, not from reading the full pages. Snippets are usually accurate but can be truncated or stale.

**Confidence labels used**
- **VERIFIED** — stated in a search snippet of a primary/official source, and consistent across ≥2 sources where possible.
- **LIKELY** — single-source snippet, plausible, uncontradicted.
- **UNVERIFIED** — not found, or found only by inference. Do **not** put on the site without Mati confirming.

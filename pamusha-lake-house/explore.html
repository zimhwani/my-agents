Lakes, beach, slow days.

Ninety miles of beach over the dune, a Ramsar wetland at the door, and the whole Gippsland Lakes Coastal Park to walk. Here's how we'd spend a week.

Getting here

Three hours from Melbourne. A long way from everything else.

Take the M1 and Princes Highway east to Sale, then follow the signs south to Seaspray and turn onto Shoreline Drive. The Honeysuckles is a few minutes along the road between Seaspray and Golden Beach. Fill up and shop in Sale; the general store at Seaspray covers the essentials after that.

  • Melbourne ~3 h
  • Sale ~40 min
  • Seaspray ~10 min
  • Golden Beach ~15 min

Ninety Mile Beach

Over the dune, and it's yours.

One of the longest uninterrupted beaches in the world runs right past the house. Walk the track over the dune for sunrise, long walks and surf fishing for salmon, tailor and flathead.

It's an open surf beach with strong rips, so swim between the flags at Seaspray in patrol season and paddle rather than swim elsewhere. Sunset walks are the safer, better option anyway.

  • Short walk from the house
  • Patrolled at Seaspray in summer
  • Surf fishing
Lake Reeve & the Gippsland Lakes

A lake for birds, and for doing nothing.

Lake Reeve is a shallow, saline wetland listed under the Ramsar convention. It isn't a boating or swimming lake, and that's the point: at dawn and dusk it turns to glass, and in spring it fills with tens of thousands of migratory waders, with fairy terns nesting along the edges.

For paddling, sailing and fishing, Lake Victoria at Loch Sport and Lake Wellington near Sale are the big-water options, both an easy day trip.

Birdwatching

Bring binoculars. Black swans, pelicans, terns and, if you're lucky, a wedge-tailed eagle.

Paddling

Kayak Merriman Creek at Seaspray, or Lake Victoria from Loch Sport.

Walking

The Honeysuckles-to-Seaspray coastal walk through the Coastal Park.

Seasons

There's no wrong time.

Dec – Feb

Summer

Patrolled beach, long dusks, the hot tub at midnight. The Australia Day surf-fishing carnival at Golden Beach.

Mar – May

Autumn

Calm lake mornings, the Easter fishing competition, and wineries at harvest. Our favourite.

Jun – Aug

Winter

Storm-watching from the deck, the fire lit by four, pub lunches in Sale. Clearest skies of the year.

Sep – Nov

Spring

Wildflowers in the heath, migratory birds filling Lake Reeve, and joeys along the fence line.

Wildlife & safety

The bush is close and the wildlife lives here too. Go gently and everyone gets along.

Snakes

Snakes are around in the warmer months. Stick to paths, keep doors closed, and never try to move one. If one's in the house, keep your distance and call a snake catcher: TODO: local snake catcher number.

If someone is bitten: call 000, keep them still, and apply a firm pressure bandage over the bite and up the limb. Don't wash the bite or try to catch the snake.

Kangaroos & wildlife

Please don't feed anything. Drive slowly on Shoreline Drive at dawn and dusk when kangaroos are on the road. Keep pets on a lead in the Coastal Park.

Water & beach

Watch children around the lake edge and the hot tub. Ninety Mile Beach is an open surf beach with strong rips: swim between the flags at Seaspray in patrol season, and never at the unpatrolled beach near the house.

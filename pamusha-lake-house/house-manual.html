Heating & cooling

The air conditioning remote is on the kitchen bench. Each bedroom also has its own heater for the night.

Please switch the air conditioning and the heaters off when you head out for the day, and before you check out.

The fireplace

The wood burner has a built-in fan that pushes the heat into the room.

  1. Light the fire as usual.
  2. Flick the switch labelled FP Fan, to the right of the fireplace between the two power points. You'll hear a beep as the fan starts.
  3. The fan remote is in the kitchen top drawer, labelled.

Hot tub & fire pit

The hot tub is wood-fired. Light it after lunch and it's ready by dark.

Lighting the hot tub

  1. Check the tub is full to the fill line before you light anything. Never light it empty.
  2. Firewood and kindling are TODO: location.
  3. Open the air vent, build a small fire in the stove, and add larger pieces once it's going.
  4. Keep feeding it. It takes around 2–3 hours to come up to temperature; stir the water and check with the thermometer.
  5. When it's hot enough, let the fire die down and close the vent.

The instructions on the stove take precedence over these. Never leave the fire unattended. Cover on whenever the tub isn't in use. No glass near the tub. Children always supervised.

Fire pit

Keep fires small, never leave one unattended, and put it out fully before bed.

Fire season: The Honeysuckles is in a bushfire-prone area. No outdoor fires on Total Fire Ban days or during a declared Fire Danger Period unless permitted. Check CFA restrictions before you light anything.

The deck at night

Blankets are in the games room. Turn the deck lights off for the best view of the sky, and give your eyes ten minutes.

Wildlife & safety

Pamusha backs onto Lake Reeve and borders a nature reserve, so the neighbours have feathers and tails.

Kangaroos, birds & foxes

Kangaroos and foxes come to the edge of the property most evenings around 7 to 8 pm, and the birdlife is constant. Keep a safe distance, wear proper shoes when you walk the grounds, and keep an eye on kids playing outside. Please don't feed anything.

Snakes

Snakes are about in the warmer months. Stick to paths, keep doors closed, and never try to move one.

If someone is bitten: call 000, keep them still, and apply a firm pressure bandage over the bite and up the limb. Don't wash the bite or try to catch the snake.

Water & beach

Watch children around the lake edge and the hot tub. Ninety Mile Beach is an open surf beach with strong rips: swim between the flags at Seaspray in patrol season, and never at the unpatrolled beach near the house.

Fire safety

The fire extinguisher is under the kitchen sink. On high fire-danger days, follow the VicEmergency app and leave early if advised.

Driving

Take Shoreline Drive slowly at dawn and dusk. Kangaroos cross without looking.

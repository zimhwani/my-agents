Our story

A house named for the feeling of arriving.

Pamusha means “at home” in Shona. It was the word we reached for the first evening we sat on this deck and watched Lake Reeve go still.

We built the house around that feeling: room for nine, two places to gather, one hot tub heated by wood you light yourself. Ninety Mile Beach is a walk away. The stars arrive on their own.

Come as you are; leave a little slower.

Fire and stars

Light it after lunch. It's ready by dark.

The hot tub is wood-fired and seats eight, tucked beside the lake with the fire pit and the swings a few steps away. There's no dial to turn. You feed the fire, you wait, and the evening slows down to meet you.

Bring a jumper. The deck is best after the stars come out.

The water

Lake at your feet. Ocean over the dune.

The house sits on Lake Reeve, a long, shallow wetland inside the Gippsland Lakes Coastal Park. It is a place for birds and stillness rather than boats: in spring the shallows fill with migratory waders, and kangaroos graze the fence line at dusk.

Over the dune is Ninety Mile Beach, a short walk from the door. Seaspray's general store and patrolled beach are about ten minutes by car, Sale about forty, and Melbourne around three hours.

  • Short walk to the beach
  • Seaspray ~10 min
  • Sale ~40 min
  • Melbourne ~3 h

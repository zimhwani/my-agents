The house

Room to be together, and apart.

Four bedrooms, two bathrooms, two living rooms and a long undercover deck, most of them facing the water. Built for nine, with the bush on one side and Lake Reeve on the other.

Bedrooms

Everyone gets a good night.

Sleeps 2 · King · Ensuite

The primary suite

A king bed, a walk-in robe, and a parents' retreat that opens straight onto the undercover deck, so the first thing you see is the lake.

The ensuite has twin rainfall showers and hand-held showers, with room for two to get ready without negotiating.

Sleeps 2 · Queen

The lake room

A calm queen room with soft morning light. Good wardrobe space for a long stay, and close to the main bathroom.

Sleeps 3 · Queen + king single bunk

The bunk room

A triple bunk with a queen mattress on the bottom and a king single on top. Three kids, or two adults and one, or the cousins who insist on sharing.

Sleeps 2 · Queen

The garden room

A queen room looking into the bush. Birdsong at dawn, and the quietest corner of the house at night.

Living, kitchen & bathrooms

Two places to gather. One long table.

Kitchen & main living

Open plan, with gas appliances, a dishwasher, and a French-door fridge plumbed for ice and filtered water. Cook for nine without anyone getting in the way.

  • Gas cooktop & oven
  • Dishwasher
  • Plumbed ice & filtered water

The second living room

A separate lounge for films, board games, or reading while the rest of the house is loud. Two living areas means nobody has to go to bed early to get some quiet.

  • Separate lounge
  • Free Wi-Fi

Main bathroom

A large freestanding bath, two rainfall showers and hand-held showers. A separate toilet sits next door so the bath is never on the critical path.

  • Freestanding bath
  • Twin rainfall showers
  • Separate toilet

Ensuite

Off the primary suite: dual rainfall showers, hand-held showers, and enough bench for two.

  • Dual rainfall showers
  • Walk-in robe
Outdoors

The lake, the fire, the sky.

Everything outside is arranged around one idea: staying out later than you meant to.

Wood-fired hot tub

Seats eight. Light it after lunch and it's ready by dark. Tucked beside the lake, a few steps from the fire.

Fire pit

Connected to the hot tub and the swings, so the whole evening happens in one place.

The deck

Large and undercover, facing the water. Guests tell us it's the best seat in the house once the stars are out.

Swings

By the water, beside the fire pit. For kids first thing, and adults last thing.

Garden & bush

Garden and bushland on the edge of the Coastal Park. Kangaroos at the fence at dusk; keep an eye out for the yellow-tailed black cockatoos.

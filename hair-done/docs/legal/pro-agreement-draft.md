**Between** Hair Done (ABN 45 281 131 814, Melbourne, Victoria) ("Hair Done", "we") and the person
setting up a pro profile in the Hair Done app ("you").

## 1. What this covers

You use Hair Done to be found and booked by clients for hair, nails, makeup, lashes and brows done at
the client's place. This agreement sets out how that works between you and us. The Terms apply too;
this adds the detail for pros. [Should this be accepted with a tick in pro onboarding, with the date
and version recorded? The app doesn't record that yet.]

## 2. You're an independent business

- You're not our employee, contractor or agent, and nothing here makes you one. [Check against the
  Fair Work Act's independent contractor tests, and the 2024 "employee-like" platform worker rules.]
- You decide whether to take a booking, how you do the work, what you charge, your hours and where
  you travel.
- An ABN is optional; if you have one, it goes on your clients' receipts. You're responsible for your own tax, GST if you're registered,
  super, licences, permits, insurance and the products and tools you use.
- You won't say you work for Hair Done or are endorsed by us.

## 3. Getting listed

Clients see you once you've:
- finished your profile (services and prices, travel area and fee, hours, photos of your work);
- sent your photo ID and a selfie, and we've checked them;
- set up payouts with Stripe.

## 4. Bookings

- **Prices.** You set your own service prices and one travel fee. Clients see every line before they book.
- **Requests.** Unless you've turned on instant book, a booking comes to you as a request. Answer
  within two hours or it lapses and the client can book someone else.
- **Instant book** is for verified pros with a reliability score of 70 or more.
- **On the day.** Mark each step in the app: on my way, here, started, done. The client sees them.
- **Messages.** Use the in-app messages for anything about a booking. Phone numbers are shared once
  a booking's confirmed.

## 5. Money

- **Our fee** is 12% of what you charge for a booking (your services plus your travel fee). It comes
  off before your payout.
- **Clients pay us a separate $3 booking fee.** It isn't taken from your price.
- **Tips** are yours in full. We take no fee from them.
- **When you're paid.** The client pays when you mark the job done, or we charge their card 12 hours
  later. If the client taps "Something wrong?" first, we hold off the charge while we look, for up
  to 48 hours.
- **Payouts** go to your bank through Stripe, under the Stripe Connected Account Agreement (which
  includes the Stripe Services Agreement). By setting up payouts you agree to those, and to us
  sharing information about you and your bookings with Stripe. When money lands depends on Stripe
  and your bank.
- **GST.** Hair Done isn't registered for GST. If you are, your prices include GST and you're
  responsible for it. [Does Hair Done need to issue anything to GST-registered pros, e.g. a monthly
  statement of fees? Is our 12% a fee for a service supplied to the pro?]
- **Refunds and disputes.** If a client reports a problem, we look at the booking and messages,
  talk to you both, and decide what's fair. That can include a full or part refund of what you
  were paid. [How is a refund taken back from a pro once paid out: from the next payout?]

## 6. Cancelling

- **If you cancel** a confirmed booking, the client isn't charged and you aren't paid.
- **If the client cancels:**

  | When | You get |
  |---|---|
  | 24 hours or more before | nothing (no charge) |
  | under 24 hours, or while you're on your way | half your price, less our 12% |
  | after you've arrived, or a no-show | your full price, less our 12% |

- **No-shows.** You can mark one only once you're at the address, have messaged the client in the
  app, and have waited 15 minutes past the later of the start time and the time you arrived.

## 7. Reliability

Late answers, lapsed requests and cancellations count against your reliability, which isn't
public. If it drops too low, instant book turns off; lower still, we may stop showing you until
we've talked.

## 8. Standards and safety

- Do the work with due care and skill, as the Australian Consumer Law requires.
- Follow hygiene and safety practice for your trade, including patch tests where they're standard.
- Be respectful in clients' homes and in messages. No harassment, discrimination or unsafe behaviour.
- If you feel unsafe, leave and tell us.

We can pause or remove a profile over safety concerns, fraud, repeated cancellations, serious
complaints or breaking this agreement. Where we reasonably can, we'll tell you why and let you respond.

## 9. Your photos and profile

Only post photos of your own work, with the client's permission if they're in the photo. You keep
ownership. You give us a free, non-exclusive licence to show them in the app and to promote Hair
Done, for as long as they're on your profile.

## 10. Privacy

We handle your details (ID, ABN, bank details through Stripe, location and messages) under our
privacy policy. ID photos are deleted once checked. Use clients' details only for their booking,
and never contact them outside the app for anything else.

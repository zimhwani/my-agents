Support

The quickest way to reach us is Help in the app (You, then Help). A person replies, usually the same day. Or email hello@hairdonenailsdone.app.

Booking

Pick a pro, the services and a time she's free, then the address. Some pros confirm straight away; others get a request and have two hours to say yes. If she doesn't answer, or says no, the hold on your card is released and nothing is charged.

To move a booking, open it and choose a new time. You can do this up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, message her in the app.

Paying

When you book, we place a hold on your card for the total. Nothing is charged yet. When she marks the job done, you can pay in the app and add a tip if you like. If you don't, we charge your card 12 hours later. Tips go to the pro in full.

Your receipt is on the booking (Get receipt), and we email it to you when you pay.

Cancelling

Cancelling a request, or a booking 24 hours or more before it starts, costs nothing. Less than 24 hours before, it's half her price; after she's arrived, it's her full price plus the $3 booking fee. The app shows the exact amount before you confirm. The full rules are in the terms.

Something wrong?

Tap "Something wrong?" on the booking before you're charged, and we'll hold off the charge while a person looks. Then tell us what happened through Help. We'll talk to both of you and work out what's fair, which can include a refund.

Your account

Sign in with Apple or with your email (we send a six-digit code). To change your details, go to You. To delete your account, go to You, then Delete account. Once nothing's left to finish or pay, your account and your personal details are deleted. Our privacy policy says what we keep for tax and legal reasons, and for how long.

For pros

Set up from the same app: You, then Set up Pro mode. You'll need photo ID, a selfie, an ABN and a bank account for payouts (through Stripe). Our fee is 12% of what you charge for a booking; tips are yours in full. The terms cover the rest.

2. What Hair Done is

Hair Done is a marketplace. It connects people who want hair, nails, makeup, lashes or brows done at home ("clients") with independent professionals who come to them ("pros").

Pros run their own businesses. They set their own services, prices, travel area and hours, and they do the work. Hair Done doesn't employ pros and doesn't provide beauty services itself. When you book, your agreement for the service is with the pro. We provide the app, take payment on the pro's behalf, check pros before they're listed, and help when something goes wrong.

4. Booking and paying (clients)

Prices

Each pro sets her own prices and one travel fee, shown on her profile before you book. We add a $3 booking fee to each booking. You'll see every line, and the total, before you pay.

The hold

When you book, we place a hold on your card for the total. Nothing is charged yet. If the booking is more than six days away, we save your card and place the hold six days before.

When you're charged

When the pro marks the job done, you can pay in the app straight away, and add a tip if you like. If you don't, we charge your card 12 hours after she marks it done. If something's wrong, tap "Something wrong?" on the booking before then and we'll hold off the charge while we look.

Tips

Tips are optional and go to the pro in full. We take nothing from them.

Requests and instant book

Some pros confirm bookings straight away. Others get a request and have two hours to say yes. If she doesn't answer in time, or says no, the hold is released and you're not charged.

Moving a booking

You can move a booking to another time she's free, from the booking, if it's 24 hours or more away. Inside 24 hours, message her to sort it out, or cancel under the rules below.

Payment processing

Payments are processed by Stripe. Hair Done never sees or stores your full card number.
